## 1. Introduction

### What is TPT?

The **TPT (Tagbilaran Polytechnic Test)** is an online examination management system designed to streamline the entrance examination process for students applying to Tagbilaran Polytechnic programs.

### What Can You Do With This System?

**For Students/Applicants:**
- Register and create an account
- Fill out application forms
- Submit payment for examination fees
- Download examination permit
- Select your preferred test center and schedule
- View examination results

**For Administrators:**
- Manage examination schedules
- Review and approve/reject payment submissions
- Manage test centers and available slots
- Upload and publish examination results
- Generate reports

## 2. Getting Started

### System Requirements

To use the TPT system, you need:
- A computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone
- Internet connection
- A web browser (Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Microsoft Edge)
- A Google account (for registration)

### Accessing the System

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to the TPT website (provided by your institution)
3. You will see the login page

## 3. For Applicants (Students)

### Step 1: Registration

**How to Create an Account:**

1. On the login page, click **"Sign in with Google"**
2. Select your Google account or enter your Google email and password
3. Allow the required permissions
4. You will be automatically registered and logged into the system

> **Note:** Your Google email will be your username for this system.

---

### Step 2: Getting Started with Your Application

After logging in for the first time:

1. You will see the **Applicant Home** page
2. Click the **"Get Started"** button
3. The system will check if there is an active examination
4. If available, you will proceed to fill out your application

---

### Step 3: Filling Out the Application Forms

You need to complete **three (3) forms**:

#### Form 1: Personal Information

Fill in the following details:
- **Applicant Type:** Select if you are a Freshmen or Transferee
- **Full Name:** First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, Extension (if any)
- **Address:** Present Address and Permanent Address
- **Phone Number:** Your active mobile number
- **Date of Birth:** Your birthdate
- **Place of Birth:** City/Municipality where you were born
- **Age:** Will be calculated automatically
- **Sex:** Male or Female
- **Other Details:** Tribe, Religion, Nationality, Citizenship
- **Photo:** Upload your 2x2 ID photo (clear, recent photo)

Click **"Save"** after completing this form.

#### Form 2: School Information

Fill in the following details:
- **School Name:** Name of your previous school (Senior High School)
- **School Address:** Location of your school
- **Level:** Senior High School, ALS, etc.
- **GWA:** Your General Weighted Average
- **Strand:** Your academic strand (STEM, ABM, HUMSS, etc.)
- **School Type:** Public or Private
- **Year Graduated:** Year you graduated

Click **"Save"** after completing this form.

#### Form 3: Program Choices

Select your preferred programs in order of priority:
- **1st Choice:** Your most preferred program
- **2nd Choice:** Your second preferred program
- **3rd Choice:** Your third preferred program

> **Tip:** Programs are grouped by Campus. Review all available programs before making your selection.

Click **"Save"** after completing this form.

#### Submitting Your Application

After completing all three forms:
1. Review your information carefully
2. Click the **"Submit Application"** button
3. Once submitted, you will proceed to the payment step

> **Important:** Make sure all information is correct before submitting. Some information may not be editable after submission.

---

### Step 4: Payment Submission

After submitting your application:

1. You will see the **Payment Page**
2. Pay the examination fee through the designated payment channel (GCash, bank transfer, etc.)
3. Keep your **payment receipt**

**To submit your payment proof:**

1. Enter the **Reference Number** from your payment receipt
2. Click **"Choose File"** to upload your proof of payment
   - Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, PDF
   - You can upload multiple files if needed
3. Make sure the receipt is clear and readable
4. Click **"Submit Payment"**

After submission, your payment will be reviewed by the administrator.

---

### Step 5: Waiting for Approval

After submitting your payment:

1. Your status will show **"Waiting for Approval"**
2. The administrator will review your payment proof
3. You will receive an email notification about your status

**Possible outcomes:**

**If APPROVED:**
- You will receive an approval email
- You can now access your examination permit
- You can select your test center and schedule

**If REJECTED:**
- You will receive an email explaining the reason
- Common reasons: blurry receipt, wrong reference number, incomplete payment
- You can resubmit your payment with correct documents

---

### Step 6: Downloading Your Permit

Once your payment is approved:

1. Go to **Applicant Home**
2. Click **"View Permit"** or **"Download Permit"**
3. Your permit contains:
   - Your **Examinee Number** (important!)
   - Your name and photo
   - Examination details
   - QR Code for verification

**Print your permit** or save it on your phone to present on examination day.

---

### Step 7: Selecting Your Test Center

After your payment is approved:

1. Click **"Select Test Center"**
2. View available test centers (campuses)
3. See available time slots (dates and times)
4. Select your preferred:
   - **Test Center:** Choose your preferred location
   - **Schedule:** Choose your preferred date and time
5. Click **"Confirm Selection"**

Your permit will be updated with:
- Room Number
- Seat Number
- Examination Date and Time

> **Important:** Select your slot early! Slots are limited and assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.

---

### Step 8: Taking the Examination

On your examination day:

1. **Bring your permit** (printed or digital)
2. Bring a valid ID
3. Arrive at least 30 minutes early
4. Go to your assigned **Room** and **Seat Number**
5. Present your permit for verification
6. Take the examination

---

### Step 9: Viewing Your Results

After the examination:

1. Wait for the administrator to upload and publish results
2. You will be notified when results are available
3. Log in to the system

**To view your results:**

1. Complete the **Survey** (required before viewing results)
2. Select your **Preferred Courses**
3. Click **"View Result"**

Your result will show:
- Scores for each subject (Math, English, Filipino, Science, Social Studies)
- Raw Scores (actual points earned)
- Standard Scores (Stanine scale 1-9)
- Score Interpretation:
  - 9: Outstanding
  - 8: Very High
  - 7: Above Average
  - 6: High Average
  - 5: Average
  - 4: Low Average
  - 3: Below Average
  - 2: Low
  - 1: Very Low

You can also **download your result as PDF**.

## 4. For Administrators

### Accessing the Admin Panel

1. Log in with your administrator account
2. You will be directed to the **Admin Dashboard**

### Admin Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows:
- Total number of users
- Total examinations
- Total programs
- Active examination details
- Slot statistics (Total, Occupied, Available)
- Number of applicants with permits

---

### Managing Examinations

**To create a new examination:**

1. Go to **Examinations**
2. Click **"Create Examination"**
3. Fill in examination details
4. Save the examination

**To activate an examination:**

1. Find the examination in the list
2. Click **"Set as Active"**
3. Only one examination can be active at a time

> **Note:** Students can only apply when there is an active examination.

---

### Reviewing Payment Applications

**To review pending payments:**

1. Go to **Manage Examination** > **Applications**
2. Find applicants with **"Pending Payment"** status
3. Click on an applicant to view details

**To approve a payment:**

1. View the payment reference number
2. View/download the uploaded proof documents
3. Verify the payment is valid
4. Click **"Approve"**
5. The system will:
   - Generate an examination permit with a unique examinee number
   - Send an approval email to the applicant

**To reject a payment:**

1. Click **"Reject"**
2. Enter the reason for rejection (e.g., "Receipt is not readable")
3. Confirm rejection
4. The system will:
   - Notify the applicant via email
   - Allow the applicant to resubmit payment

---

### Managing Test Centers and Slots

**To add a test center:**

1. Go to **Manage Examination** > **Slots**
2. Click **"Add Test Center"**
3. Enter the test center name and campus

**To add exam slots:**

1. Select a test center
2. Click **"Add Slot"**
3. Set the date and time
4. Set the capacity (number of examinees)
5. Save the slot

**To activate/deactivate slots:**

- **Active slots:** Visible and available for student selection
- **Inactive slots:** Hidden from students

Click the toggle button to change slot status.

---

### Uploading Examination Results

**To upload results:**

1. Go to **Results** > **Upload Results**
2. Select the examination
3. Prepare your Excel file with the following columns:
   - examinee_number
   - math_raw_score
   - math_standard_score
   - english_raw_score
   - english_standard_score
   - filipino_raw_score
   - filipino_standard_score
   - science_raw_score
   - science_standard_score
   - social_studies_raw_score
   - social_studies_standard_score
   - total_raw_score
   - total_standard_score
4. Click **"Upload"** and select your Excel file
5. The system will import all results

**To publish results:**

1. After uploading, go to **Examinations**
2. Find the examination
3. Enable **"Show Results"** toggle
4. Students can now view their results

---

### Managing Campuses and Programs

**Campuses:**
1. Go to **Campuses**
2. Add, edit, or delete campus information

**Programs:**
1. Go to **Programs**
2. Add programs linked to specific campuses
3. Set programs as "Offered" or "Not Offered"

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### For Applicants

**Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?**
A: Since you log in with Google, use Google's password recovery at accounts.google.com.

**Q: Can I edit my application after submitting?**
A: Some information may be locked after submission. Contact the administrator if you need to make changes.

**Q: My payment was rejected. What should I do?**
A: Check the rejection reason in your email, fix the issue (e.g., upload a clearer receipt), and resubmit your payment.

**Q: How do I know if my payment is approved?**
A: You will receive an email notification. You can also check your status by logging in to the system.

**Q: What if all exam slots are full?**
A: Contact the administrator. They may open additional slots or test centers.

**Q: When will results be available?**
A: Results are released after the administrator uploads and publishes them. You will be notified when available.

**Q: I can't view my results. Why?**
A: You must complete the survey first before viewing results.

### For Administrators

**Q: How do I activate a new examination?**
A: Go to Examinations, find the exam, and click "Set as Active". Remember that only one examination can be active at a time.

**Q: An applicant submitted wrong information. Can I edit it?**
A: Direct database edits may be required for significant changes. Contact your technical support team.

**Q: How do I add more exam slots?**
A: Go to Manage Examination > Slots, select or create a test center, then add new slots with your desired capacity.

## 6. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in with Google | Clear browser cache, try incognito mode, or use a different browser |
| Page not loading | Check internet connection, refresh the page |
| Upload failed | Check file size and format (JPG, PNG, PDF only) |
| Can't see "Get Started" button | No active examination or all slots are full |
| Permit not showing exam details | You haven't selected a test center slot yet |
| Results not available | Administrator hasn't published results yet |

### Browser Recommendations

For best experience, use:
- Google Chrome (latest version)
- Mozilla Firefox (latest version)
- Microsoft Edge (latest version)

### Clearing Browser Cache

If experiencing issues:

**Chrome:**
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
2. Select "Cached images and files"
3. Click "Clear data"

**Firefox:**
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
2. Select "Cache"
3. Click "Clear Now"
